1. Introduction

Smart Scanner is an Android application for scanning documents, cropping images, recognizing text, creating and editing PDF files, and saving or sharing documents. This policy explains how information is handled.

2. Document Storage

Smart Scanner is designed to store saved scanned documents and PDF files locally on the user's device. The app does not intentionally upload these documents to a cloud server operated by the developer.

3. Camera and Gallery

Users can capture document images or select images from their device. Smart Scanner uses Google ML Kit Document Scanner and image-cropping functionality. Temporary files may be created during scanning and processing.

4. Text Recognition (OCR)

Smart Scanner uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition to recognize document text and suggest filenames. A temporary image is created for this process, and the app attempts to delete that temporary OCR image afterward.

5. PDF Creation and Editing

Smart Scanner allows users to create PDFs from scanned images and perform supported PDF editing operations using document data available to the application.

6. Saving and Sharing

Users can save PDFs to a destination they select using Android's document picker and share PDFs through Android's sharing functionality.

If users select a cloud storage provider or another app, that provider may process, store, or upload the document according to its own privacy practices.

7. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Smart Scanner integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The current development version uses Google's official test advertisements, which do not generate revenue.

Google advertising technology may process information such as IP addresses, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information, depending on SDK behavior, device settings, and applicable privacy choices.

Smart Scanner also uses Google ML Kit for document scanning and text recognition. Google services may process technical information associated with their operation.

Read the Google Privacy Policy .

8. Device Permissions

Smart Scanner includes permissions supporting network connectivity, advertising-related services, and certain Android system operations. Camera capture, image selection, saving, and sharing use supported Android components.

9. Data Security

Smart Scanner is designed to keep saved documents on the user's device unless they choose to export or share them. Users should protect their devices and exercise care when handling sensitive documents.

10. Children's Privacy

Smart Scanner is not specifically designed for children. The developer will review applicable privacy, consent, and advertising requirements before enabling production advertisements.

11. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when Smart Scanner's features, third-party services, or data practices change. The effective date will be revised accordingly.
