1. Introduction

Smart Scanner is an Android application for scanning documents, cropping images, recognizing text, creating and editing PDF files, and saving or sharing documents. This policy explains how information is collected, used, shared, retained and protected.

2. User Accounts

Smart Scanner does not require users to create an account or sign in to access its functionality.

3. Document Storage

Smart Scanner is designed to store saved scanned documents and PDF files locally on the user's device. The app does not intentionally upload these documents to a cloud server operated by the developer.

4. Camera and Gallery

Users can capture document images or select images from their device. Smart Scanner uses Google ML Kit Document Scanner and image-cropping functionality. Document scanning is performed on the device. Temporary files may be created during processing.

5. Text Recognition (OCR)

Smart Scanner uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition to recognize document text and suggest filenames. Text recognition is performed on the device. A temporary image may be created for this process, and the app attempts to delete that image afterward.

Google ML Kit may transmit technical usage and performance metrics to Google for service maintenance, troubleshooting, improvement and abuse prevention. According to Google's ML Kit documentation, document inputs and recognition results are processed locally rather than uploaded to Google for recognition.

6. PDF Creation and Editing

Smart Scanner allows users to create PDFs from scanned images and perform supported PDF editing operations using document data available to the application.

7. Saving and Sharing

Users can save PDFs to a destination they select using Android's document picker and share PDFs through Android's sharing functionality.

If users select a cloud storage provider or another application, that provider may process, store or upload documents according to its own privacy practices. Android backup services may also handle app data depending on device settings and backup configuration.

8. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Smart Scanner integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). The current test version uses Google's official test advertisements, which do not generate advertising revenue.

Google's advertising SDK can automatically collect and share the following categories of information:

  • Approximate location: General geographic information inferred from the device's IP address.
  • App interactions: Information about application launches, taps and advertising interactions.
  • Diagnostics: Technical information about application and SDK performance.
  • Device or other identifiers: Android advertising identifiers, app set identifiers and other applicable identifiers.

This information may be processed and shared by Google's advertising services for advertising, measurement, analytics, fraud detection and security.

Smart Scanner does not request Android precise location or approximate location permissions in the current release. However, an approximate geographic location may be inferred from an IP address.

Users may manage or delete their advertising identifier through supported Android privacy settings. These controls do not necessarily stop all SDK data collection.

For more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Privacy Information
  • Google ML Kit Terms and Privacy
  • Google Mobile Ads Data Disclosure

9. Device Permissions

Smart Scanner includes permissions supporting network connectivity, advertising-related services and certain Android system operations. Camera capture, image selection, saving and sharing use supported Android components.

10. Data Security

Smart Scanner is designed to keep saved documents on the user's device unless users choose to export or share them, or device backup settings apply.

According to Google's documentation, user data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is protected in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

Users should protect their devices with appropriate security measures and exercise care when storing or sharing sensitive documents. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Saved documents are retained locally until users delete them or the relevant application data or storage is removed.

Users can remove saved documents using the application's document management features. Separately exported copies must be managed at their respective storage locations.

Temporary processing files may remain until application or Android cleanup occurs.

Information collected by third-party Google services is retained according to Google's applicable data retention policies. The developer does not directly control Google's retention periods.

Smart Scanner currently does not provide a dedicated mechanism for requesting deletion of information collected by third-party SDKs. Users may consult Google's privacy controls and retention information.

Google Data Retention Policy | Google Data and Privacy Controls

Users should back up important documents before removing application data or uninstalling the app, because locally saved documents may be lost.

12. Children's Privacy

Smart Scanner's declared target audience is adults aged 18 and older. The application is not directed toward children.

Applicable advertising privacy and consent requirements will be reviewed before enabling production advertisements.

13. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when Smart Scanner's features, third-party services or data practices change. The effective date will be revised accordingly.
